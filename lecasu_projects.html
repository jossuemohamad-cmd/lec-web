INCUBOX 268,2 kWpGAPI

INCUBOX 2, GAPI - Cabo Delgado, Nampula and Niassa

The INCUBOX initiative provides digital learning environments for youth entrepreneurship and technical development in remote regions of Mozambique. LECASU has supplied reliable photovoltaic systems that power modular training units in Cabo Delgado, Nampula, and Niassa, enabling the uninterrupted operation of computers, connectivity systems, lighting, and digital education equipment. The project directly contributes to social inclusion, technical capacity building, and sustainable community development.

Reliable energy infrastructure, engineered for African conditions and built for long-term performance.
